# Privacy

MG Candy Blast keeps your progress, settings and nickname **only on your own device** (browser `localStorage`).
It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server. It does not send your data anywhere and collects no personal information.
Clearing your browser data, or **Settings → Reset Progress**, deletes the saved game.

This is a plain-language summary written by the developer; it has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
Contact: see the About screen in the game.
